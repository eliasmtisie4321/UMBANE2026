## 1. Executive Summary

**Umbane CRM** is a centralized Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
and Business Operations Platform designed for businesses involved in
electricity services, meter installations, electrical contracting and
property management.

The platform brings customer records, property information, electricity
meter records and essential business operations into one system. It
helps businesses organize their workflows, manage customer
relationships, track service requests and monitor day-to-day activities.

Umbane CRM is intended to help small and medium-sized businesses move
away from disconnected spreadsheets, paper records and manual
administrative processes.

## 7. Business Objectives

1.  Simplify customer and property information management.
2.  Improve the organization of electricity meter and installation
    records.
3.  Reduce repetitive manual administration.
4.  Improve the tracking and resolution of customer support requests.
5.  Help businesses monitor sales, invoices and inventory.
6.  Improve visibility into staff responsibilities and operational
    progress.
7.  Build a maintainable platform that can grow with business needs.

## 10. Product Principles

Umbane CRM development will be guided by the following principles:

-   **Usability:** Keep everyday workflows clear and straightforward.
-   **Security:** Protect business and customer information through
    appropriate authentication and access controls.
-   **Accuracy:** Maintain consistent records and meaningful links
    between customers, properties and meters.
-   **Reliability:** Build and test features so that core workflows
    behave consistently.
-   **Maintainability:** Use an architecture that can be understood,
    tested and improved.
-   **Practical integration:** Introduce APIs and external services
    where they solve a real business need.
-   **Responsive design:** Support users accessing the platform on
    desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
